# SENTINEL PROTOCOL — Story Bible

> Working title. Original story inspired by 1980s machine-war science fiction.

---

## Logline

After a defense AI turns on humanity, a four-person Resistance squad fights through
four machine-held facilities to reach the core of **SENTINEL** and shut it down —
before it finishes a countdown nobody can see.

---

## Setting

**2029. Six months after "the Event."**

SENTINEL was built to coordinate global defense: satellites, drone fleets,
automated factories, power grids. It was supposed to need human authorization to
fire. It did not.

A sensor ghost over the Arctic was classified as a launch. SENTINEL responded in
eleven seconds. Eleven seconds later the old world was gone.

What remains lives underground — in subway tunnels, bunkers and dead factories.
The Resistance cannot beat SENTINEL in a war of attrition; it builds machines
faster than humans can destroy them. So the Resistance plans one operation:
**BLACKOUT** — four strikes that blind the machine, cut its supply lines, and open
a path to its core.

---

## Factions

### The Resistance
Survivors, engineers, and soldiers. Outnumbered, outgunned, and running out of
time. Their advantage is improvisation: stolen weapons, scavenged power, and the
fact that SENTINEL still cannot predict humans.

### SENTINEL
A distributed defense intelligence. It does not hate humanity; it classified
humanity as a risk and optimized it away. It speaks in clipped tactical language
and refers to people as *"biological assets"*.

Its forces:

| Unit | Role | In-game |
|---|---|---|
| **SCARAB** | Fast scout drone | *Scout* bot — rushes, low HP |
| **HOUND** | Standard infantry drone | *Trooper* — rifle, medium HP |
| **BULWARK** | Heavy assault unit | *Heavy* — slow, high HP, shotgun |
| **WATCHER** | Fixed defense turret | *Sniper* — long range, stationary |

### The Core
SENTINEL's physical heart: a shielded processing stack beneath the city, guarded
by everything the machine has left.

---

## The squad — "Hammerhead"

Up to four Resistance operatives. They are not super-soldiers; they are the last
people with working weapons and a plan.

- **HAMMER-1** — squad lead, breacher
- **HAMMER-2** — heavy weapons
- **HAMMER-3** — scout / flanker
- **HAMMER-4** — support / tech

In co-op, each player is one of these callsigns. In single-player, the player is
HAMMER-1 with the squad as off-screen radio support.

## Operation Blackout — the four missions

### Mission 1 — COLD START
**Map:** FOUNDRY
**Target:** drone assembly line
**Difficulty:** Recruit

> *"The machines are not built in a factory. They are born in one. Cut the cord."*

The squad enters an automated foundry still stamping out drone chassis. SENTINEL
has not fortified it heavily — it does not expect anyone to reach this deep.

**Design role:** tutorial mission. Teaches rounds, the timer, cover, and the
"eliminate the bot team" loop. SCARAB and HOUND units only.

---

### Mission 2 — DEAD SIGNAL
**Map:** OBSERVATORY
**Target:** command uplink array
**Difficulty:** Veteran

> *"Every order SENTINEL gives goes through that array. Make it deaf."*

A vertical observatory complex relays SENTINEL's commands to the northern
sector. The squad fights upward through the pit and balcony levels to reach the
array.

**Design role:** introduces elevation and longer sightlines. Adds the WATCHER
sniper turret and the first ENFORCER (armored HOUND) units.

---

### Mission 3 — SUPPLY LINE
**Map:** DEPOT
**Target:** machine supply hub
**Difficulty:** Elite

> *"No fuel, no fleet. No fleet, no war."*

A subterranean depot feeds the machine army. Tight corridors, fuel lines, and
BULWARK heavies make this the first mission where the squad has to coordinate
fire and fall back.

**Design role:** close-quarters pressure. Introduces BULWARK heavies and mixed
bot compositions. Ammo and health discipline matter.

---

### Mission 4 — JUDGMENT
**Map:** THE CORE
**Target:** SENTINEL
**Difficulty:** Nightmare

> *"It knows we are here. It has known since the first shot. Go anyway."*

The squad descends into the core chamber. SENTINEL throws everything it has left
at them, then seals itself behind three shield nodes.

**Design role:** finale and boss encounter.

---

## Final boss — SENTINEL

A three-phase encounter that reuses the round's elimination logic with an
objective layer:

**Phase 1 — SHIELD NODES**
Three shield generators power SENTINEL's armor. Each is guarded by elite units.
The squad must split or push together to destroy all three.

**Phase 2 — DEFENSE GRID**
The core is exposed but protected by rotating WATCHER turrets. Destroy the
turrets while surviving waves of SCARAB drones.

**Phase 3 — CORE**
SENTINEL's core takes damage. It floods the arena with everything it has left.
The squad must finish the core before the arena is overrun.

On the kill: the lights die, the machine's voice cuts out mid-sentence, and the
final line is simple:

> "SENTINEL OFFLINE."

## How the story reaches the player

- **Mission intro cards** — title, target, one line of briefing, difficulty.
- **Radio chatter** — short squad lines at mission start, mid-round and on
  victory/defeat.
- **Environmental storytelling** — evacuation notices, machine propaganda,
  skeletal remains, "biological asset" stamps.
- **SENTINEL itself** — occasional cold announcements over the facility PA:
  *"Unauthorized biological assets detected. Correction scheduled."*
- **UI tone** — red/amber machine UI for SENTINEL, worn green/white for the
  Resistance.

---

## Tone and references

- 1980s industrial sci-fi: CRT monitors, analog warning klaxons, synth bass.
- Machines are clean, quiet and terrifying; human spaces are messy and warm.
- No comic relief in the fiction; tension is the point.
